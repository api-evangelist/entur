Entur AS is a Norwegian state-owned technology company ("et statlig eid teknologiselskap") that works to make it easier to choose public transport across Norway. It runs the national journey planner at entur.no and its app, sells tickets, and operates the national data platform for public transport. Through Entur for Developers it publishes open services (journey planner, geocoder, real-time vehicle and mobility data) and open data for free, plus partner services that require authentication.

**Areas:** Company · Public Transport · Journey Planning · Mobility · Open Data · Norway · Transit · GraphQL

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Entur: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
